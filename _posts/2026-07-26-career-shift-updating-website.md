It has been about 1 year since I properly updated this website.

A lot has changed professionally. I have moved from government research and official statistics into customer research at the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors, or more commonly known RICS. It's not a career change; more of a shift I would call it.

Before joining RICS, I worked on the Gambling Survey for Great Britain at the Gambling Commission. Prior to that, I spent several years at the Office for National Statistics, working on major economic surveys and operations.

Although I have changed broad sector much of the work stays the same. A mix of surveys, interviews, analysis and other research methods depending on the question. I started descrining myself as a *methodological magpie* and then defintely fits.

Alongside my main role, I have continued to publish and collaborate academically. I'm still intersted in social media, but I've mainly stopped that. Instead several months ago I was flattered to be invited to join the Nature Medicine Commission on Quality Health Information for All, as a member on one of their working groups. This international initiative is examining how the quality and use of health information can be improved. I contribute my experience in mixed-methods research and large-scale surveys.

I have now updated this website to better reflect this combination of customer research, government experience and international academic collaboration.
