It has been about a year since I properly updated this website.

A lot has changed professionally. I have moved from government research and official statistics into customer research at the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors, more commonly known as RICS. It is not a career change; I would call it more of a shift.

Before joining RICS, I worked on the Gambling Survey for Great Britain at the Gambling Commission. Prior to that, I spent several years at the Office for National Statistics, working on major economic surveys and operations.

Although I have moved into a different sector, much of the work stays the same: a mix of surveys, interviews, analysis and other research methods, depending on the question. I started describing myself as a *methodological magpie*, and that definitely still fits.

Alongside my main role, I have continued to publish and collaborate academically. I am still interested in social media, but I have mainly stepped back from that area. Several months ago, I was flattered to be invited to join the *Nature Medicine* Commission on Quality Health Information for All as a member of one of its working groups. This international initiative is examining how the quality and use of health information can be improved. I contribute my experience in mixed-methods research and large-scale surveys.

I have now updated my website to better reflect these change, hopefully with more short articles from all the fun I've been having at RICS!
