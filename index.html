Hi, I’m Naiyan. Or as some people call me, Nay.

I am a mixed-methods researcher bringing together statistics, social research, user research, and data science to improve products, services, and decisions. I currently work as a Customer Research Specialist at the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors, delivering qualitative and quantitative research and helping embed a more insight-led approach across the organisation. Previously, I worked on major survey programmes at the Gambling Commission and Office for National Statistics. Alongside my professional work, I publish academic research on social media, health communication, and research ethics, and contribute to international work on public health information.
